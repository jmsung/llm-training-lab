# llm-training-lab

A learning lab that runs two pipelines end to end and reports what each step costs and buys. Stage 1 trains a small chat model with nanochat: pretraining, supervised fine-tuning, then reinforcement learning. Stage 2 fine-tunes a small open model (Qwen) with LoRA through SFT, DPO and GRPO on a task with an automatic scorer, and reports before/after numbers.
